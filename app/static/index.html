    How to read the chart

    • Each dot is a block of time — a day, a month, or a year depending on what you picked above.
    • Good-for-outdoors hours is a simple tally: how many hours in that block passed every rule listed above.
    • Click any dot to see the actual hours behind it. On a day, we show all 24 hours from each of the region’s sample points so you can see exactly when the weather turned.

    Where the numbers come from

    • We use hourly historical weather data from the global ERA5 record — the same archive meteorologists use for climate analysis, updated to within about five days of today.
    • Each region is the average of its sample points.
    • UV is estimated from the sun’s angle and cloud cover — close to what a consumer UV index would report, but not a direct sensor reading.
