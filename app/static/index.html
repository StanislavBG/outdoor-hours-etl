How to read the chart

  • Each dot is a block of time — a day, a month, or a year depending on what you picked above.
  • Good-for-outdoors hours is a simple tally: how many hours in that block passed the four-rule check (sunny-enough, comfortable, safe UV, not pouring).
  • Click any dot to see the actual hours behind it. On a day, we show all 24 hours from each of the 3 sample neighborhoods so you can see exactly when the weather turned.

Where the numbers come from

  • We use hourly historical weather data from the global ERA5 record — the same archive meteorologists use for climate analysis, updated to within about five days of today.
  • Each county is represented by three neighborhoods (San Jose / Sunnyvale / Mountain View in CA; Bellevue / Redmond / Woodinville in WA), then averaged.
  • UV is estimated from the sun’s angle and cloud cover — close to what a consumer UV index would report, but not a direct sensor reading.
